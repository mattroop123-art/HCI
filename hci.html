Course overview

Human Computer Interfaces

Human Computer Interfaces explores how to design technology around the people who use it. The course begins with user research and human perception, attention, and memory, then applies those foundations to navigation, layouts, forms, and interactive prototypes. You will learn to test designs with users, interpret evidence, improve accessibility, and consider the ethical and privacy consequences of interface decisions. By the end, you should be able to move from an observed user need to a coherent prototype, explain your design choices, and evaluate whether the interface helps people accomplish their goals.
