Course overview

Data Structures and Algorithms 2

Data Structures and Algorithms 2 develops the tools you need to solve computational problems efficiently and explain why your solutions work. Beginning with algorithm analysis and recursive problem solving, the course moves through search trees, heaps, hashing, and graph algorithms before tackling dynamic programming, network flow, and computational complexity. You will practice tracing algorithms, selecting data structures, proving correctness, and comparing time and space costs. By the end, you should be able to turn a problem’s constraints into a justified algorithmic approach and recognize when an exact solution may be impractical.
